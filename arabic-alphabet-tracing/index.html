Free Arabic Alphabet Tracing Worksheets for Kids

Arabic is written from right to left, and every letter changes shape depending on where it sits in a word. These free printable pages let your child try our method: dotted guides on the center line, so the pencil moves in one smooth stroke, exactly like real handwriting — not tracing around an outline.

What’s inside (4 pages)

  • Warm-up lines and curves
  • A letter page: name, shapes, picture
  • A practice page on the center line
  • A coloring / find-and-circle page
Download free PDF Parent guide (free)

Tips for parents

  • Start every row on the right: Arabic is written right to left.
  • Trace the dots first, then write the letter alone in the empty boxes.
  • Say the letter name out loud each time — sound and shape stick together.
