Arabic is written from right to left, and many letters change form depending on their position in a word. Children follow dotted guides along the center of each stroke, rather than tracing around letter outlines. Download four free pages and try the method at home.

Tips for parents

  • Start every row on the right: Arabic is written right to left.
  • Trace the dots first, then write the letter on its own in the empty boxes.
  • Say the letter name out loud each time, so sound and shape go together.
