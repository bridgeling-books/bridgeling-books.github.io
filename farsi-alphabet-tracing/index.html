Free Farsi (Persian) Alphabet Tracing Worksheets

Persian uses 32 letters, including پ چ ژ گ that do not exist in Arabic. Our free sample shows the letter, its shapes, a practice page and a coloring page with a real Persian word — validated by a native speaker.

What’s inside (4 pages)

  • Warm-up lines and curves
  • A letter page: name, shapes, picture
  • A practice page on the center line
  • A coloring / find-and-circle page
Download free PDF Parent guide (free)

Tips for parents

  • Rows start on the right, the way Farsi is really written.
  • Dots are part of the letter: add them after the main stroke.
  • Ten minutes a day beats one long session a week.
