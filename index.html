A gentle way to learn, one stroke at a time

1 · See the model

A large, clear letter with its name, its forms and a picture word.

2 · Follow the stroke

Dotted guides sit on the center of each stroke — children write the letter, they don't trace around it.

3 · Write on their own

Empty boxes, reviews and coloring pages turn practice into confidence.
